## 1. Club Rules

1. **Respect.** Members and guests must treat other members, guests and staff with courtesy. Harassment, discrimination, aggressive behaviour or abusive language is not tolerated.
2. **Dress code.** Smart attire is expected in the Century Dining Room and meeting rooms. Management may refuse entry or ask a person to change. Specific section dress codes (gym, tennis, snooker) apply in those areas.
3. **Admission.** Members must carry proof of membership. Members are responsible for the conduct of their guests and for registering them at reception.
4. **Guests.** Guests must be signed in by a member or hold a valid booking. Management may limit guest numbers and frequency.
5. **Bookings.** All table, hall, event and food requests are subject to club approval. Confirmation occurs only after payment. Bookings may be cancelled in line with the Cancellation Policy.
6. **Conduct in rooms.** Hirers must leave rooms tidy, follow the capacity limits, and must not move or damage equipment. Hirers are liable for damage caused by them or their guests.
7. **Food and drink.** Outside food and drink may not be brought onto the premises without management permission. Alcohol is served responsibly; the club may refuse service.
8. **Mobile phones and recording.** Phones must be used discreetly in dining areas. Photography or filming of other people without consent is not allowed. The club may photograph events for the gallery (see Privacy Policy).
9. **Smoking and vaping** are not permitted indoors.
10. **Health and safety.** Follow staff instructions, fire notices and emergency procedures. Report accidents or hazards to reception.
11. **Property.** The club is not liable for lost or stolen personal property.
12. **Fees and financial status.** Members must keep subscriptions up to date. Members who are not financial may be suspended and lose booking and sponsorship rights.
13. **Sponsorship.** A member who sponsors an applicant must act honestly and in good faith. A sponsor may withdraw support before the club's decision.
14. **Discipline.** Breach of these rules may result in a warning, suspension or termination of membership, at the club's discretion following a fair process.
15. **Changes.** The club may amend these rules and will publish changes on the website.

**4. Prices and payment.** Prices are in pounds sterling. Prices for halls, tables, events and food include VAT. Membership entrance, category and section fees are not subject to VAT. Payments are processed by Stripe; the Club never stores your card details.

**5. Cancellations and refunds.**

- Halls: 100% refund 7 or more days before; 50% between 48 hours and 7 days; no refund under 48 hours.
- Tables: 100% refund 24 or more hours before; none after.
- Events: 100% refund 7 or more days before; none after, unless the Club can resell your place.
- Personal food orders: 100% refund 24 or more hours before. Party orders: 100% at 72 or more hours, 50% at 24-72 hours, none under 24 hours.
- If the Club cancels, you receive a full refund.
- No-shows are not refunded. Repeated no-shows may lead to booking restrictions.
- Refunds are made to the original payment method.

**6. Membership.** 6.1 Membership is by application, sponsorship by two eligible members, interview and approval by the Club. The Club's decision is final, subject to the re-application route below. 6.2 The application fee (where charged) is **not refundable**, whatever the outcome. 6.3 Unsuccessful applicants are informed in writing and may re-apply, at no charge, on providing satisfactory evidence that the reason for refusal has been addressed. 6.4 Entrance and section fees are payable before activation and are not refundable once membership is active. 6.5 Membership runs for one year and is renewed by annual or monthly payment. Annual subscriptions are not refunded part-way through the term. Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled before the next payment date. 6.6 If a payment fails, the Club may suspend membership after a grace period of 14 days. A suspended member cannot use member rates, make bookings or sponsor applicants. Membership may be terminated after prolonged suspension. 6.7 Members must follow the Club Rules.

**10. Changes and termination.** The Club may update these terms and the platform. Continued use after changes means you accept them. The Club may suspend or close accounts that breach these terms.

**Who we are.** Charlton Exclusive Club (Unit 6, Block 3 Woolwich, Dockyard Industrial Estate, Woolwich Church St, Charlton, London SE18 5PQ) is the data controller. Contact: [info@charltonexclusiveclub.co.uk](mailto:info@charltonexclusiveclub.co.uk). ICO registration: \[ICO REGISTRATION NUMBER\].

**What we collect**

- *Account and contact data:* name, email, phone, address, date of birth, password (hashed).
- *Membership data:* application form, category and sections, sponsor details, interview notes and outcome, membership status, join date, payment status.
- *Booking and order data:* requests, dates, party size, purpose, special requests, check-in records.
- *Dietary and health information:* dietary requirements you give on food orders. This may reveal health or religious belief and is **special category data**; we process it only with your explicit consent, and only to fulfil your order.
- *Payment data:* processed by Stripe. We keep transaction references, amounts and status, never full card numbers.
- *Gallery photos:* images from club events.
- *Technical data:* IP address, device/browser data, cookies (see Cookie Policy).
- *Communications:* contact forms and emails.

**Why we use it, and our lawful basis**

| Purpose | Basis |
| --- | --- |
| Process applications, run membership, bookings and orders | Contract / steps before contract |
| Take payments, issue receipts, keep accounts | Contract; legal obligation |
| Screening, interviews and sponsorship checks | Legitimate interests (maintaining a members' club) |
| Service emails (approvals, receipts, renewal reminders) | Contract / legitimate interests |
| Security, fraud prevention, audit logs | Legitimate interests |
| Dietary information | Explicit consent |
| Event photos in the gallery | Legitimate interests; objection honoured at any time |
| Analytics and marketing cookies | Consent |

**Sharing.** We share data only with service providers acting on our instructions: Stripe (payments), Zoho ZeptoMail (transactional email), our hosting provider (uploaded files are stored on the club's own server), and professional advisers. Sponsors see only the status of applicants they sponsor. Committee members see applications they review. We do not sell your data.

**International transfers.** Where providers process data outside the UK, we rely on adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement / Addendum.

**Retention**

- Active member records: duration of membership plus 6 years.
- Financial records: 6 years.
- Unsuccessful applications: 12 months after decision (then deleted or anonymised), unless you re-apply.
- Contact enquiries: 12 months.
- Booking records: 6 years (financial).
- Gallery photos: until removed.
- Audit logs: 6 years.

**Your rights.** You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or request a portable copy. Contact [info@charltonexclusiveclub.co.uk](mailto:info@charltonexclusiveclub.co.uk); we respond within one month. We cannot delete records we must keep by law (e.g. financial records). If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113).

**Photos.** Admin selects which photos appear and whether they are public or members-only. To ask for a photo to be removed, email [info@charltonexclusiveclub.co.uk](mailto:info@charltonexclusiveclub.co.uk) and we will act promptly.

**Security.** Encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, MFA for staff, audit logging. We will notify the ICO and affected individuals of qualifying breaches.

**Changes.** We will post updates here with a new version date.
