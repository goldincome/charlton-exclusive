Membership

Joined by introduction

Membership is by application, supported by two sponsoring members and a short interview with our committee.

    Choose your category and at least two sections.
  2. 2Be sponsored
    Two members who have belonged for three years or more confirm your nomination.

    A relaxed interview, then your welcome.
